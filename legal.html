Service Description & Pricing

Univers Franck provides an online French learning web application covering complete A1–C1 content (with C2 in preparation), including reading texts, audio, structured grammar lessons, verb conjugation, and spaced repetition (FSRS). The application currently provides a French immersion interface with FR/JP translations and explanations; full English translations are in preparation.

  • Monthly plan: $6.99 / month (or ¥780 / month in JPY)
  • Annual plan: $59 / year (or ¥6,800 / year in JPY)

Exact tax-inclusive prices are displayed on the Polar.sh checkout screen prior to payment confirmation. Private 1-on-1 students with Timothy receive full access and their personal space (★) included without a separate subscription.

Immediate Digital Delivery

Full access to the Univers Franck Web App is unlocked automatically upon payment confirmation by Polar.sh (typically within seconds).

3 · Privacy Policy

Privacy & Data Protection

Data Stored in Firebase

To authenticate your account and synchronize your learning progress across devices, Univers Franck stores only the minimum data required in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore):

  • Your email address and optional display name
  • Your subscription / app access status (e.g., active plan, Polar customer/subscription reference)
  • Your learning progress (studied words, FSRS review intervals, reading and exercise history)

Payment Data

Univers Franck never receives or stores your credit card numbers or banking details. All payments, invoices, and tax records are processed securely by Polar.sh (as Merchant of Record) under Polar’s own privacy policy.

Analytics & Communications

The marketing website (universfranck.com) uses Google Analytics to measure aggregated site traffic. Emails sent to tim@universfranck.com are used solely to reply to your inquiry or arrange lessons. We never sell or rent your personal data.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your account and stored learning data at any time by writing to tim@universfranck.com.
